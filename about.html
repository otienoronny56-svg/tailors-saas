Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering custom garments and partnering with Stitch & Styles Kenya.

How long does a custom 2-piece or 3-piece suit take to be tailored in Nairobi?
Standard custom suit creation typically takes between 5 to 10 business days depending on fabric availability and fitting stage. Express 72-hour turnaround services are also available with participating verified tailors on the marketplace.
How do I save and attach my measurements for custom African wear or dresses?
You can create custom measurement profiles directly in your Client Dashboard under "Measurement Vault". When opening a garment modal or sending an inquiry, check the "Attach my saved custom measurements" box to send your exact sizing automatically to the tailor.
Are the tailors on Stitch & Styles Kenya verified for quality and reliability?
Yes! All tailors and fashion houses undergo identity, workshop, and craftsmanship verification before receiving the "Verified Shop" badge. You can view client ratings, completed order history, and portfolio photos on every tailor's digital storefront.
What payment options are supported for custom garments and SaaS subscriptions?
We support seamless M-Pesa payments, bank transfers, and major credit cards. Client payments for custom orders are processed through secure milestone payments linked to order stage progress.
Can I track my order progress in real-time?
Absolutely! Once your order is placed, you can follow every stage live on your client dashboard — from Order Placed → Fabric Sourcing → Cutting & Stitching → Quality Check → Ready for Collection/Delivery. You'll also receive email and SMS notifications at each milestone.
How do I register my tailoring shop on the platform?
Simply click "Sign Up" and choose the Tailor/Provider role. You'll be guided through a quick onboarding process where you set up your shop profile, upload your portfolio, and choose your subscription plan. New shops get a free trial period to explore all features.
